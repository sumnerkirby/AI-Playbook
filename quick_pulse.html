Monthly check-in · about 1 minute

Monthly AI check-inthree or four questions, once a month

AI is often added one feature at a time, in software the business already uses. Three questions a month identify new AI soon after it appears. A business with staff gets a fourth, about AI accounts for anyone who joined or left. A year of answers produces a current list of your AI tools and a dated record showing that the business monitors its use of AI.

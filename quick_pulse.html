Monthly check-in · about 1 minute · prototype

Monthly AI check-inthree questions, once a month

AI is often added one feature at a time, in software the business already uses. Three questions a month identify new AI soon after it appears. A year of answers produces a current list of your AI tools and a dated record showing that the business monitors its use of AI.

Prototype. Check-in wording version ; new tools are rated with quick check rules , not yet verified for publication.

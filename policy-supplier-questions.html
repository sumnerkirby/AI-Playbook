Supplier questionsWhat to ask an AI provider

Seventeen questions to ask an AI supplier before trusting it with your information, with notes on how to interpret the answers. Many providers answer most of these questions in published documents, so check those first.

Before you start

  • Use these questions for any tool that will handle customer, staff or confidential information (Levels 2 and 3 in the tool approval procedure).
  • Check the provider’s trust or security page, privacy policy and terms first, and then send only the questions those documents do not answer.
  • A supplier’s refusal to answer basic questions is itself useful information.

Why ask

You cannot inspect how an AI product works, but you can ask what it does with your information and compare the answers across products. Large providers do not negotiate their terms with small businesses, so for most readers these questions serve to choose rather than to negotiate: between products, and between the free and business versions of the same product.

The questions

Supplier questions · copy into an email

Subject: Questions about [product name]

Hello,

We are considering using [product name] at [business name] for [purpose]. Before we do, please answer the questions below, or tell us where the answers are published.

Our data

  1. Is anything we type, upload or connect used to train or improve your models, or anyone else’s? If so, can we turn this off, and is it off by default on the plan we would use?
  2. How long do you keep what we put in and what the product produces, and can we shorten that or delete it ourselves?
  3. Do any of your staff or contractors see our data, and in what circumstances?
  4. Which other companies process our data on your behalf, including whoever provides the underlying AI model?
  5. In which countries is our data stored and processed?
  6. Where is your company headquartered, and under which country’s law can a government require you to disclose our data?
  7. Do any subprocessors outside [our country] receive our data, or have access to it? Please list them.

Security

  1. What independent security certifications or audits do you have, for example ISO 27001 or SOC 2?
  2. Can we manage users centrally, require two-step sign-in, and remove access when someone leaves?
  3. How, and how quickly, would you tell us about a breach affecting our data?

The AI itself

  1. Which AI model or models does the product use, and will you tell us if that changes?
  2. What are the product’s known limitations, and what do you recommend customers check for themselves?
  3. If the product can connect to other systems or take actions, what control do we have over what it can reach and do?

Contract and leaving

  1. Do you offer a data processing agreement, and can we see it before we sign up?
  2. How much notice do you give before changing your terms, or the way you use our data?
  3. If we stop using the product, how do we export our data, and how do you confirm it has been deleted?
  4. What responsibility do you accept if the product causes us a loss, for example by exposing our data?

Thank you,
[name], [business name]

Reading the answers

The goal is clear answers, not perfect ones. A vague answer to a question that matters to you calls for a follow-up question, or a comparison with a competing product.

TopicReassuringCause for concern
TrainingOff by default for business customers, and stated in the contractOnly a setting each user has to find, or “we may use your data to improve our services”
Keeping dataClear time limits, and you can delete things yourself“As long as necessary”, with no way to delete
Where data is heldA named country or region for storage and processing, the law that governs it, and where each subprocessor isNo answer on where data is held or which law applies
Other companiesA published list of the companies that process your dataThe supplier will not say who
SecurityAn independent certification or audit you can ask to seeGeneral reassurance only
BreachesA committed timescale for telling you, written into the contract“Promptly”, with no commitment
ChangesAdvance notice of significant changes to termsTerms that can change at any time without notice
LeavingExport in a standard format, and deletion confirmedNo export, or no confirmation of deletion

Keep the answers with the tool’s entry on your AI list, and ask again when the product or its terms change significantly.

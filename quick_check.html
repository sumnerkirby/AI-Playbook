Quick check · about 10 minutes

Quick AI checkfor small businesses

The check has two parts, after three questions about your business that the whole site uses. The first part asks six yes-or-no questions about the situations most likely to cause serious harm. The second asks you to select the everyday tasks in which your business uses AI, and sorts each task into one of three groups: acceptable, needs a check, or stop.

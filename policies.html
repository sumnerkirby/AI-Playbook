AI PoliciesTemplates for small businesses

These templates are the documents that put the playbook’s steps into practice. Each is short and written in plain language, and the parts you need to fill in are highlighted. Copy a template into your own document, change anything that does not fit, and delete what you do not need.

You do not need all of these at once. Every business that uses AI should have the first three: someone named as AI lead, an acceptable use policy, and an incident plan. Add the others as your use of AI grows: the tool approval procedure and supplier questions once you pay for AI tools, the human review standard once AI output reaches customers, and the rules for connected AI as soon as any tool can act on your behalf.

Keep them together in one folder that everyone can find, and give each a version number and a date. None of them is legal advice. If you work in a regulated industry, or handle health, financial or children’s information, have a qualified advisor review your versions.

Steps 4–6

Step 4 · a table to adapt Data handling rules Three groups of information: never, approved business tools only, and anywhere. Included in the data guide. Stay in control

Steps 7–9

Step 7 · 1–2 pages Rules for AI that can act Least access, a person’s approval for anything that sends, pays, deletes or changes records, and spending limits. Step 9 · checklist Quarterly AI review checklist The 30-to-60-minute review that keeps your list, your policy and your settings current.

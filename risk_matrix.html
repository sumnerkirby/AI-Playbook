Risk overviewall of your AI risk in one place

The traffic light on your AI list shows whether a tool may be used for a particular task. This page shows where the risk lies across all the tools you use, and which risks to address first. A tool can be allowed and still carry high impact, and this page keeps such tools in view.

Prototype. Risk rules version . Positions are suggested from your tool-check answers. The likelihood thresholds are a starting point for testing, not validated values. There is no single overall score, because one number would hide which risks matter.

PrivacyWhat this site keeps, and how to delete it

This page lists everything the site saves in your browser, how long it is kept and how to delete it. It also explains what the page address holds, and what to do on a computer that other people use.

In short

  • Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts and no analytics.
  • Answers stay in this browser. The quick check and the business profile also keep answers in the page address, so they appear in your browser history and in any link you copy.
  • The site’s host, GitHub Pages, receives ordinary page requests, as it would for any website.
  • You can delete everything the site saved in this browser with one button.

Where your answers go

In this browser

The tools save your answers in this browser’s storage, on this device. Some are kept until you delete them. Others last only until you close the tab. The table below lists each one. Another browser, or another device, does not see them.

In the page address

The quick check keeps your answers in the page address, after the # character, so that the browser’s back button and a bookmark work. The business profile can do the same when you share it. Browsers do not send the part after the # to any server, but they do keep the address in your browser history, and history can be synced to your other devices. A link you copy from these pages contains your answers, and anyone who opens it can read them. Text you type yourself, such as Something else on the quick check, is never put in the address.

The site’s host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser asks GitHub for it, as it would for any website, and GitHub receives the ordinary details of that request, such as your IP address and the page name. The part of the address after the # is not part of that request.

How the browser holds the site to this

Every page tells your browser not to let its scripts connect to any server, and not to load scripts, styles, fonts or images from other websites. The browser enforces this, so it does not rely on our word alone. Links you choose to follow still open other websites, and those websites are not told which page you came from.

What is saved, and for how long

Each item is saved under its own name, called a key, which starts with sb-ai-playbook:. The last column shows what is saved in this browser now.

Each tool also has its own delete button: Delete it for a saved quick check or policy, Clear my profile, Delete all check-ins, and Delete from the list for each use on the AI list.

Delete everything

This deletes everything this site saved in this browser, for every tool. It cannot be undone. To keep your AI list or your check-ins, download a backup from those pages first.

It does not remove pages from your browser history. To remove quick check answers kept in old addresses, clear this site from your browser’s history.

On a shared office computer

  • Use a private window (called incognito in some browsers). The browser deletes what the site saved when you close that window.
  • Or press Delete everything above when you finish, and close the tab.
  • Anyone who uses the same browser can open the tools and see what you saved, and can find the quick check in the browser history.
  • On a computer you do not control, the quick check and the policy are saved when you finish them. Print or download what you need, then use Delete everything before you leave.

Sharing a link

A quick check link or a profile link contains your answers. Anyone who opens it can read them. If you only want to point someone to the tool, use Copy the address without answers, or send them the home page.

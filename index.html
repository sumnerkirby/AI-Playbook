AI Playbookfor small businesses

Your staff are almost certainly using AI already. Some of it they’ve chosen, like a chat assistant for drafting emails, and some of it has quietly arrived inside software you already pay for. The trouble starts when nobody knows what’s being used, what information goes into it, or who would notice if it went wrong.

This playbook is nine steps for getting a handle on that: who’s responsible, which tools are in use, what the rules are, how your data is protected, and what happens when something goes wrong. It’s written for businesses without a security team or a compliance department.

The steps are based on the AI Risk Management Framework from the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. Each one quotes the passages it draws on, word for word, so you can check how we’ve read them. Nothing you enter on this site leaves your browser.

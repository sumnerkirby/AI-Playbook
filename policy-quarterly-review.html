Quarterly reviewA checklist for keeping it current

A checklist for the short review that keeps everything else up to date. It takes 30 to 60 minutes once a quarter, led by your AI lead.

Before you start

  • Put all four dates for the year in the calendar now.
  • Have your AI list, your policy and your incident log open while you go through it.
  • Keep each completed checklist. Together they’re a record of how you manage AI, if a customer or insurer ever asks.

Why every quarter

AI products change faster than most software. Suppliers add features, change their terms and switch on new capabilities, often without much notice, and your team will keep finding new tools. A review every three months is frequent enough to catch those changes before they cause trouble, and infrequent enough that it actually happens.

Quarterly review checklist · copy or print

[Business name]: Quarterly AI review

Date: ____________   Led by: ____________   Also present: ____________

Tools

  • ☐ Asked the team whether they’ve started using any new AI tools or features
  • ☐ Checked the software we already pay for, for AI features that have been added or switched on
  • ☐ Added new tools to the AI list, and started approval for any that aren’t approved
  • ☐ Removed tools nobody uses any more, and closed their accounts
  • ☐ Checked that nobody who has left still has access to our AI tools

Suppliers and settings

  • ☐ Checked for changes to the terms or privacy policies of approved tools
  • ☐ Confirmed that training is still switched off wherever it should be
  • ☐ Checked spending on usage-billed AI services against their limits
  • ☐ Looked through what connected AI tools have done since the last review

Incidents

  • ☐ Read through the incident log since the last review
  • ☐ Confirmed every incident has been closed and its follow-up done
  • ☐ Considered whether any pattern calls for a change to our rules or some training

Policy and people

  • ☐ Re-read the AI policy and asked whether it still matches how we actually work
  • ☐ Made any changes needed, updated the version number, and told the team what changed
  • ☐ Checked that new starters have read the policy
  • ☐ Checked whether any law, customer contract or insurance requirement affecting our AI use has changed

Notes

What has changed since last time: ____________________________________________

Actions agreed (what, who, by when): ____________________________________________

Next review: ____________

Adapting it

  • Drop the lines that don’t apply. If no tool can act on its own, you don’t need the connected AI line yet.
  • Add anything your business needs to check regularly, such as the sample of chatbot conversations from your human review standard.
  • Keep it short. If the review regularly runs over an hour, some of it probably belongs in the day-to-day work of the AI lead.

Quarterly reviewA checklist for keeping it current

A checklist for the short review that keeps the other documents current. The review takes 30 to 60 minutes once a quarter and is led by your AI lead.

Before you start

  • Schedule all four review dates for the year now.
  • Have your AI list, your policy and your incident log available during the review.
  • Keep each completed checklist. Together, the checklists form a record of how you manage AI, which you can show a customer or insurer on request.

Why every quarter

AI products change faster than most software. Suppliers add features, change their terms and switch on new capabilities, often with little notice, and staff continue to find new tools. A review every three months is frequent enough to catch these changes before they cause problems, and infrequent enough that it is likely to be carried out.

Quarterly review checklist · copy or print

[Business name]: Quarterly AI review

Date: ____________   Led by: ____________   Also present: ____________

Tools

  • ☐ Asked staff whether they have started using any new AI tools or features
  • ☐ Checked the software we already pay for, for AI features that have been added or switched on
  • ☐ Added new tools to the AI list, and started approval for any that are not approved
  • ☐ Removed tools that are no longer used, and closed their accounts
  • ☐ Checked that no former employee still has access to our AI tools

Suppliers and settings

  • ☐ Checked for changes to the terms or privacy policies of approved tools
  • ☐ Confirmed that training is still switched off wherever it should be
  • ☐ Checked spending on usage-billed AI services against their limits
  • ☐ Reviewed what connected AI tools have done since the last review

Incidents

  • ☐ Read the incident log entries since the last review
  • ☐ Confirmed that every incident has been closed and its follow-up completed
  • ☐ Considered whether any pattern calls for a change to our rules or additional training

Policy and people

  • ☐ Re-read the AI policy and checked whether it still matches how we work
  • ☐ Made any necessary changes, updated the version number, and told staff what changed
  • ☐ Checked that new employees have read the policy
  • ☐ Checked whether any law, customer contract or insurance requirement affecting our AI use has changed

Notes

What has changed since last time: ____________________________________________

Actions agreed (what, who, by when): ____________________________________________

Next review: ____________

Adapting it

  • Remove the lines that do not apply. If no tool can act on its own, you do not need the connected AI line yet.
  • Add anything your business needs to check regularly, such as the sample of chatbot conversations from your human review standard.
  • Keep it short. If the review regularly takes more than an hour, some of the work probably belongs in the AI lead’s routine duties.
